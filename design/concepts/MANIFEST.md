## Shared visual rules

- Baseline: 1920x1080, with a verified 1280x720 layout.
- Warm ember orange communicates player action, heat, and human machinery.
- Cool cyan communicates corridor anomalies, hostile energy, and unstable technology.
- Important gameplay silhouettes and telegraphs must remain readable without relying on color alone.
- UI layout, iconography, terminology, characters, enemies, and environments must remain original and must not reproduce another game's protected assets or interface.

## Approval gate

An image can enter the game only after it has been converted into production-ready source assets, visually reviewed in context, tested at target resolutions, and changed from `concept-only` to `approved` in `docs/ASSET-LICENSE-REGISTER.csv`.
